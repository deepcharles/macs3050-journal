### Question 2: Research question

The research aimed to find how much ultra-processed foods such as snacks, sweetened drinks, etc. make up of the diets of teenagers in Adama city, Ethiopia. It's important because the researchers talked about how little information on this topic is available for low and middle-income countries like in sub-Saharan Africa. They also talked about the overlooked consequences that come with an ultra processes, food, rich diet, such as obesity and diabetes.

### Question 3: Data

The research focussed on 713 high school students in Adama City and was collected between October and November of 2024. The data was collected by surveying 29 private schools and 15 public schools.

### Question 4: Results

- around 92% of high school students consume at least one ultra processed food a week
- students from middle income and higher families, showed a smaller consumption of UPFs
- out of all the grades that were surveyed, grade 12 students seem to be consuming the most
